AG Cars

Artificial Gravity Transportation Systems

AG Cars are a theoretical transportation system designed around electromagnetic suspension, active AI stabilization, and thrust-based propulsion.

Traditional vehicles rely on friction-based wheel systems. AG Cars propose a different model: once a vehicle is suspended above the ground, wheel traction becomes unnecessary.

Instead of transferring force through wheels, AG Cars would theoretically:

  • Suspend above the roadway using electromagnetic balancing systems
  • Use AI-assisted stabilization to maintain equilibrium
  • Propel themselves through air displacement systems such as ducted fans or electric thrust
  • Reduce rolling friction to nearly zero

Existing technologies partially supporting the theory already exist today:

  • Maglev train systems
  • Hovercraft technologies
  • Drone stabilization systems
  • eVTOL aircraft
  • Electromagnetic suspension engineering

AG Cars remain theoretical, but the concept demonstrates how future transportation systems may evolve beyond wheel-based mechanics.
